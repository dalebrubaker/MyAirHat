## Installation Sequence Comparison

### Option 1 (Integrated) - 7 Steps:
1. Cut cylinder with extra 1.5" at top
2. Serge raw edge
3. Position over hat brim
4. Fold to create integrated casing
5. Stitch single seam line
6. Cut access hole and insert wire
7. Seal access hole

### Option 2 (Two-Stage) - 8 Steps:
1. Cut and serge cylinder normally
2. Pin cylinder to hat brim
3. Stitch base attachment seam
4. Prepare bias strip for wire casing
5. Position and pin casing above attachment
6. Stitch casing (leaving wire opening)
7. Insert wire through casing opening
8. Close wire opening

Both methods result in a secure, nearly airtight connection with integrated spring steel wire tensioning system.
